As of Nov 20, 2026: Meta plans to charge $300 for the Muse Charm, according to a report by [The Information](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1038087/meta-muse-charm-ai-price), relayed by The Verge and [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/19/metas-new-muse-charm-device-may-cost-300/). Meta has not announced a price. When TechCrunch asked, a Meta spokesperson didn't deny the claim.

## What the Muse Charm is

Mark Zuckerberg revealed the Muse Charm earlier this year. The Verge describes it as a little gadget with a screen that lets you chat with your Muse AI agent. It has a fingerprint sensor, and you can tap it to talk to a Muse agent without unlocking a phone or opening an app. TechCrunch calls it a Tamagotchi-like device, about the size of a keychain, that you could carry in a pocket or possibly wear around your neck.

Zuckerberg said Meta expected to ship it "in time for the holidays in December" but gave no price at the time. The report also says it will come with a free 12-month 5G data plan from Verizon.

## What the price is compared to

TechCrunch puts $300 next to other AI wearables:

- Friend's device, which lets you text and talk to an AI persona, retails for $250.
- Amazon's Bee Frontier, which transcribes conversations and tracks tasks, lists for $75. It is on a Black Friday sale at $52.49 and has a $99/year subscription that connects it to a user's apps, such as photos and email.

TechCrunch's read is that $300 is arguably steep for a keychain-sized product, though in line with what other companies charge.

## What Meta said

The spokesperson's full statement: "We're charmed by the interest in our AI devices, and we'll have more to share soon."

## Limits

- This is one report. The Verge and TechCrunch both point back to The Information, and I haven't seen the original.
- Meta hasn't confirmed the price, the shipping date or the Verizon plan. A reported plan can change before launch.
- The articles don't say what the $300 includes, whether Muse needs a subscription, or what the device does without a phone nearby.
- I haven't used the device. This is a summary of the reporting, not a review.

## What I'd take from it

If the price holds, the Charm sits above Friend and well above Bee Frontier. TechCrunch's point about AI wearables having "a rough go of it so far" is the open question. I'd wait for Meta's own announcement before drawing conclusions.
